01 Optional inquiry alerts

Compass Marketing Advisors LLC provides optional automated account notifications to the business owners and authorized staff who use Compass CRM. Each text alerts the recipient to a new inquiry submitted through their business website. Message frequency varies with inquiry volume. This program does not include promotional texts.

Enrollment is handled directly with a Compass representative. Providing a phone number or becoming a client does not enroll anyone automatically. Visiting this page does not enroll you. Alerts begin only after sender approval and an affirmative consent record.

Read the SMS program terms and privacy policy. For assistance, email thomas@compassmarketing.ai.

02 Verbal consent script

Before enrolling a recipient during a phone or in-person conversation, a Compass representative reads the following disclosure in full, provides the links above, answers any questions, and asks the recipient to confirm the number they personally use.

Would you like to receive automated text messages from Compass Marketing Advisors LLC when your business receives a new website inquiry? These are Compass CRM account alerts, not marketing texts. Message frequency varies with your inquiry volume. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. You can also contact thomas@compassmarketing.ai. Consent is optional and is not a condition of purchasing services. Our SMS terms are at compassmarketing.ai/terms.html and our privacy policy is at compassmarketing.ai/privacy.html. Do you agree to receive these inquiry alerts at the mobile number you have confirmed?

Illustrative response only: “Yes, I agree to receive Compass CRM inquiry alerts at my confirmed mobile number.” This example is not a record of any person's consent.

If the person declines, gives an unclear answer, or has not received the disclosure, the representative does not enroll them. The representative must not treat a third party's request as the recipient's own consent.

03 Consent record

Before enabling alerts, the representative must retain an access-controlled record associated with the recipient's client account. The record includes:

  • Recipient name, business, and confirmed mobile number.
  • Date, time, and time zone of the consent conversation.
  • Method, either phone or in person, and the representative who obtained consent.
  • The disclosure version, 2026-10-11, and the recipient's actual affirmative response.
  • Any later withdrawal of consent and the date it was received.

Recipient records are private and are not published on this page. The public script documents the enrollment process; it does not establish that any particular recipient has completed it.

04 Your choices

Reply STOP to opt out of texts. You may receive one confirmation of the opt-out. Reply HELP for help, or contact thomas@compassmarketing.ai. Email requests to stop alerts must also be honored. Further alerts require renewed affirmative consent.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Keep checking your email and CRM account for inquiries. We do not sell or share your mobile information or SMS consent for third-party marketing or promotional purposes.
